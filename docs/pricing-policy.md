# Pricing and discount policy

## Standard plans
Starter: up to 5 data sources and 10 dashboards.
Growth: up to 25 data sources, unlimited dashboards and weekly onboarding calls.
Enterprise: unlimited data sources, SSO, a dedicated account manager and the uptime commitment.
List prices are maintained by finance and change quarterly. Sales must confirm current prices with finance before quoting.

## Discounts
Account executives may offer up to 10% off list price for annual prepayment.
Discounts between 10% and 20% need sales director approval.
Discounts above 20% need finance approval and a written business case.

## Onboarding services
Dashboard onboarding is scoped in weeks. A typical onboarding for three teams takes 4 to 6 weeks.
Never promise a migration timeline shorter than 2 weeks without an engineering estimate.
