# Support and service levels

## Response times
Northstar support responds to Priority 1 incidents (dashboards down for all users) within 1 hour, 24/7.
Priority 2 incidents (degraded refreshes or partial data) receive a response within 4 business hours.
Priority 3 requests (questions, minor bugs) receive a response within 2 business days.

## Escalation
Priority 1 incidents page the on-call engineer from the Data Platform team.
If an incident is not mitigated within 4 hours, the engineering manager joins the incident channel.
Customers receive a written incident summary within 5 business days of resolution.

## Service credits
Enterprise plans include a 99.5% monthly uptime commitment for dashboards.
Below 99.5%, the customer may request a 10% credit on that month's platform fee.
Credits are requested through the account manager and approved by finance.
